Our Story

Where Tradition Meets Innovation

Welcome to Seb's Garden, where culinary excellence meets cutting-edge technology. Founded with a passion for creating unforgettable dining experiences, we've revolutionized how you explore and enjoy food.

Our journey began with a simple idea: what if customers could see exactly what they're ordering, in stunning 3D detail, before it even reaches their table? This vision led us to become the first restaurant in the region to offer an interactive 3D menu experience.

Every dish at Seb's Garden tells a story. From our carefully sourced ingredients to our innovative presentation, we believe that dining should engage all your senses. Our 3D models allow you to explore each creation from every angle, building anticipation and enhancing your culinary journey.

Why 3D Menus?

Revolutionizing Dining

At Seb's Garden, we believe transparency builds trust. Our 3D menu allows you to:

  • ✅ See exactly what you're ordering
  • ✅ Explore dish composition and presentation
  • ✅ View portion sizes accurately
  • ✅ Experience dishes in Augmented Reality
  • ✅ Make informed dining decisions

This innovative approach eliminates surprises and ensures you get exactly what you expect, every time.
